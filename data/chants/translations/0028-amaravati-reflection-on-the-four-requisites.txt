[chant:0028 reflection-on-the-four-requisites]
# {Paṭisaṅkhā} yoniso cīvaraṁ paṭisevāmi,
1 Wisely reflecting, I use the robe:
# yāvadeva sītassa paṭighātāya, uṇhassa paṭighātāya,
2 only to ward off cold, to ward off heat,
# ḍaṁsa-makasa-vātātapa-siriṁsapa-samphassānaṁ
3 to ward off the touch of flies, mosquitoes, wind, burning and creeping things,
# paṭighātāya, yāvadeva hirikopina-paṭicchādanatthaṁ
4 only for the sake of modesty.
# {Paṭisaṅkhā} yoniso piṇḍapātaṁ paṭisevāmi, neva davāya, na madāya, na maṇḍanāya,
5 Wisely reflecting, I use almsfood: not for fun, not for pleasure, not for fattening,
# na vibhūsanāya, yāvadeva imassa kāyassa ṭhitiyā, yāpanāya, vihiṁsūparatiyā,
6 not for beautification, only for the maintenance and nourishment of this body, for keeping it healthy,
# brahmacariyānuggahāya, iti purāṇañca vedanaṁ paṭihaṅkhāmi,
7 for helping with the Holy Life; thinking thus, 'I will allay hunger
# navañca vedanaṁ na uppādessāmi,
8 without overeating,
# yātrā ca me bhavissati anavajjatā ca phāsuvihāro cā'ti
9 so that I may continue to live blamelessly and at ease.'
# {Paṭisaṅkhā} yoniso senāsanaṁ paṭisevāmi,
10 Wisely reflecting, I use the lodging:
# yāvadeva sītassa paṭighātāya, uṇhassa paṭighātāya,
11 only to ward off cold, to ward off heat,
# ḍaṁsa-makasa-vātātapa-siriṁsapa-samphassānaṁ
12 to ward off the touch of flies, mosquitoes, wind, burning and creeping things,
# paṭighātāya, yāvadeva utuparissaya vinodanaṁ paṭisallānārāmatthaṁ
13 only to remove the danger from weather, and for living in seclusion.
# {Paṭisaṅkhā} yoniso gilāna-paccaya-bhesajja-parikkhāraṁ paṭisevāmi,
14 Wisely reflecting, I use supports for the sick and medicinal requisites:
# yāvadeva uppannānaṁ veyyābādhikānaṁ vedanānaṁ paṭighātāya,
15 only to ward off painful feelings that have arisen,
# abyāpajjha-paramatāyā'ti
16 for the maximum freedom from disease.
