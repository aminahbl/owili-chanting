[chant:0013 supreme-praise-of-the-dhamma]
# Svākkhātat'ādiguṇa-yoga-vasena seyyo
1 Superior, through having the virtue of being well-proclaimed,
# Yo magga-pāka-pariyatti-vimokkha-bhedo
2 divided into path and fruit, study and release,
# Dhammo kuloka-patanā tada-dhāri-dhārī
3 the Dhamma upholds one from falling into bad worlds,
# Vandām'ahaṁ tama-haraṁ vara-dhammam-etaṁ
4 I worship that noble Dhamma, which carries off the darkness.
# Dhammo yo sabba-pāṇīnaṁ saraṇaṁ khemam-uttamaṁ
5 The Dhamma, which is the supreme refuge for all living beings,
# Dutiyānussatiṭṭhānaṁ vandāmi taṁ siren'ahaṁ
6 I recall second in this place, I worship it with my head,
# Dhammassāh'asmi dāso va dhammo me sāmi-kissaro
7 I am the servant of the Dhamma, the Dhamma is my lord and master.
# Dhammo dukkhassa ghātā ca vidhātā ca hitassa me
8 the Dhamma is a destroyer of suffering, a provider (of solace) and it benefits me.
# Dhammass'āhaṁ niyyādemi sarīrañ-jīvitañ-cidaṁ
9 I dedicate this body and this life to the Dhamma.
# Vandantohaṁ carissāmi dhammass'eva sudhammataṁ
10 I will live worshipping the Dhamma’s good teaching.
# Natthi me saraṇaṁ aññaṁ dhammo me saraṇaṁ varaṁ
11 For me there is no other refuge, for me the Dhamma is the best refuge!
# Etena sacca-vajjena vaḍḍheyyaṁ satthu-sāsane
12 By this declaration of the truth may the Teacher’s dispensation prosper.
# Dhammaṁ me vandamānena yaṁ puññaṁ pasutaṁ idha
13 Through my worship of the Dhamma and whatever merit is found here,
# Sabbepi antarāyā me māhesuṁ tassa tejasā
14 through the power of that, may there be no dangers for me.
# Kāyena vācāya va cetasā vā
16 By body, by speech or by mind,
# Dhamme kukammaṁ pakataṁ mayā yaṁ
17 whatever bad deeds were done by me regarding the Dhamma,
# Dhammo paṭiggaṇhātu accayantaṁ
18 may the Dhamma accept my confession,
# Kālantare saṁvarituṁ va dhamme
19 for my restraint in the future regarding the Dhamma.
