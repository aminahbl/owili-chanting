[chant:0076 khemakhema-sarana-gamana-paridipika-gatha]
# Bahuṁ ve saraṇaṁ yanti
1 To many refuges they go —
# pabbatāni vanāni ca
2 To mountain slopes and forest glades,
# Ārāma-rukkha-cetyāni
3 To parkland shrines and sacred sites —
# manussā bhaya-tajjitā
4 People overcome by fear.
# N'etaṁ kho saraṇaṁ khemaṁ
5 Such a refuge is not secure,
# n'etaṁ saraṇam-uttamaṁ
6 Such a refuge is not supreme,
# N'etaṁ saraṇam-āgamma
7 Such a refuge does not bring
# sabba-dukkhā pamuccati
8 Complete release from suffering.
# Yo ca buddhañ-ca dhammañ-ca
9 Whoever goes to refuge
# saṅghañ-ca saraṇaṁ gato
10 In the Triple Gem
# Cattāri ariya-saccāni
11 Sees with right discernment
# sammappaññāya passati
12 The Four Noble Truths:
# Dukkhaṁ dukkha-samuppādaṁ
13 Suffering and its origin
# dukkhassa ca atikkamaṁ
14 And that which lies beyond —
# Ariyañ-c'aṭṭh'aṅgikaṁ maggaṁ
15 The Noble Eightfold Path
# dukkhūpasama-gāminaṁ
16 That leads the way to suff'ring's end.
# Etaṁ kho saraṇaṁ khemaṁ
17 Such a refuge is secure,
# etaṁ saraṇam-uttamaṁ
18 Such a refuge is supreme,
# Etaṁ saraṇam-āgamma
19 Such a refuge truly brings
# sabba-dukkhā pamuccatī'ti.
20 Complete release from all suffering.
