[chant:0080 pathama-buddha-bhasita-gatha]
# Aneka-jāti-saṁsāraṁ
1 For many lifetimes in the round of birth,
# sandhāvissaṁ anibbisaṁ
2 Wandering on endlessly,
# Gaha-kāraṁ gavesanto
3 For the builder of this house I searched —
# dukkhā jāti punappunaṁ
4 How painful is repeated birth.
# Gaha-kāraka diṭṭho'si
5 House-builder you've been seen,
# puna gehaṁ na kāhasi
6 Another home you will not build,
# Sabbā te phāsukā bhaggā
7 All your rafters have been snapped,
# gaha-kūṭaṁ visaṅkhataṁ
8 Dismantled is your ridge-pole;
# Visaṅkhāra-gataṁ cittaṁ
9 The non-constructing mind
# taṇhānaṁ khayam-ajjhagā
10 Has come to craving's end.
