[chant:0081 pacchima-ovada-gatha]
# Handa dāni bhikkhave āmantayāmi vo
1 Now bhikkhus I declare to you,
# Vaya-dhammā saṅkhārā
2 Change is the nature of conditioned things;
# Appamādena sampādethā'ti
3 Perfect yourselves, not being negligent:
# Ayaṁ tathāgatassa pacchimā vācā
4 These are the Tathāgata's final words.
