[chant:0079 dhamma-garav-adi-gatha]
# Ye ca atītā sambuddhā
1 All the Buddhas of the past,
# ye ca buddhā anāgatā
2 All the Buddhas yet to come,
# Yo c'etarahi sambuddho
3 The Buddha of this current age —
# bahunnaṁ soka-nāsano
4 Dispellers of much sorrow.
# Sabbe saddhamma-garuno
5 Those having lived or living now,
# vihariṁsu viharanti ca
6 Those living in the future,
# Atho pi viharissanti
7 All do revere the True Dhamma —
# esā buddhāna dhammatā
8 That is the nature of all Buddhas.
# Tasmā hi atta-kāmena
9 Therefore desiring one's own welfare,
# mahattam-abhikaṅkhatā
10 Pursuing greatest aspirations,
# Saddhammo garu-kātabbo
11 One should revere the True Dhamma —
# saraṁ buddhāna sāsanaṁ
12 Recollecting the Buddha's teaching.
# Na hi dhammo adhammo ca
13 What is true Dhamma and what not
# Ubho sama-vipākino
14 Will never have the same results,
# Adhammo nirayaṁ neti
15 While lack of Dhamma leads to hell-realms —
# Dhammo pāpeti suggatiṁ
16 True Dhamma takes one on a good course.
# Dhammo have rakkhati dhamma-cāriṁ
17 The Dhamma guards who lives in line with it
# Dhammo suciṇṇo sukham-āvahāti
18 And leads to happiness when practised well —
# Esānisaṁso dhamme suciṇṇe
19 This is the blessing of well-practised Dhamma.
