# {Dasa ime bhikkhave} dhammā pabbajitena abhiṇhaṁ paccavekkhitabbā, katame dasa
1 Bhikkhus, there are ten dhammas which should be reflected upon again and again by one who has gone forth. What are these ten?
# Vevaṇṇiyamhi ajjhupagato'ti
2 'I am no longer living according to worldly aims and values.'
# pabbajitena abhiṇhaṁ paccavekkhitabbaṁ
3 This should be reflected upon again and again by one who has gone forth.
# Parapaṭibaddhā me jīvikā'ti
4 'My very life is sustained through the gifts of others.'
# pabbajitena abhiṇhaṁ paccavekkhitabbaṁ
5 This should be reflected upon again and again by one who has gone forth.
# Añño me ākappo karaṇīyo'ti
6 'I should strive to abandon my former habits.'
# pabbajitena abhiṇhaṁ paccavekkhitabbaṁ
7 This should be reflected upon again and again by one who has gone forth.
# Kacci nu kho me attā sīlato na upavadatī'ti
8 'Does regret over my conduct arise in my mind?'
# pabbajitena abhiṇhaṁ paccavekkhitabbaṁ
9 This should be reflected upon again and again by one who has gone forth.
# Kacci nu kho maṁ anuvicca viññū sabrahmacārī sīlato na upavadantī'ti
10 'Could my spiritual companions find fault with my conduct?'
# pabbajitena abhiṇhaṁ paccavekkhitabbaṁ
11 This should be reflected upon again and again by one who has gone forth.
# Sabbehi me piyehi manāpehi nānābhāvo vinābhāvo'ti
12 'All that is mine, beloved and pleasing, will become otherwise, will become separated from me.'
# pabbajitena abhiṇhaṁ paccavekkhitabbaṁ
13 This should be reflected upon again and again by one who has gone forth.
# Kammassakomhi kammadāyādo kammayoni kammabandhu kammapaṭisaraṇo,
14 'I am the owner of my kamma, heir to my kamma, born of my kamma, related to my kamma, abide supported by my kamma;
# yaṁ kammaṁ karissāmi, kalyāṇaṁ vā pāpakaṁ vā, tassa dāyādo bhavissāmī'ti
15 whatever kamma I shall do, for good or for ill, of that I will be the heir.'
# pabbajitena abhiṇhaṁ paccavekkhitabbaṁ
16 This should be reflected upon again and again by one who has gone forth.
# 'Kathambhūtassa me rattindivā vītipatantī'ti
17 'The days and nights are relentlessly passing; how well am I spending my time?'
# pabbajitena abhiṇhaṁ paccavekkhitabbaṁ
18 This should be reflected upon again and again by one who has gone forth.
# Kacci nu kho'haṁ suññāgāre abhiramāmī'ti
19 'Do I delight in solitude or not?'
# pabbajitena abhiṇhaṁ paccavekkhitabbaṁ
20 This should be reflected upon again and again by one who has gone forth.
# Atthi nu kho me uttari-manussa-dhammā alamariya-ñāṇa-dassana-viseso adhigato,
21 'Has my practice borne fruit with freedom or insight
# so'haṁ pacchime kāle sabrahmacārīhi puṭṭho na maṅku bhavissāmī'ti
22 so that at the end of my life I need not feel ashamed when questioned by my spiritual companions?'
# pabbajitena abhiṇhaṁ paccavekkhitabbaṁ
23 This should be reflected upon again and again by one who has gone forth.
# Ime kho bhikkhave dasa dhammā pabbajitena abhiṇhaṁ paccavekkhitabbā'ti
24 Bhikkhus, these are the ten dhammas to be reflected upon again and again by one who has gone forth.
