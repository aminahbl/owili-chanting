[chant:0006 homage-to-the-dhamma]
# Yo so svākkhāto bhagavatā dhammo
1 The Dhamma is well expounded by the Blessed One,
# Sandiṭṭhiko,
2 Apparent here and now,
# Akāliko,
3 Timeless,
# Ehipassiko,
4 Encouraging investigation,
# Opanayiko
5 Leading inwards,
# Paccattaṁ veditabbo viññūhi
6 To be experienced individually by the wise.
# Tam-ahaṁ dhammaṁ abhipūjayāmi tam-ahaṁ dhammaṁ sirasā namāmi
7 I chant my praise to this Teaching, I bow my head to this Truth.
