[chant:0077 bhadd-eka-ratta-gatha]
# Atītaṁ nānvāgameyya
1 One should not revive the past
# nappaṭikaṅkhe anāgataṁ
2 Nor speculate on what's to come;
# Yad'atītaṁ pahīnan-taṁ
3 The past is left behind,
# appattañca anāgataṁ
4 The future is un-realized.
# Paccuppannañca yo dhammaṁ
5 In every presently arisen state
# tattha tattha vipassati
6 There, just there, one clearly sees;
# Asaṁhiraṁ asaṅkuppaṁ
7 Unmoved, unagitated,
# taṁ viddhām-anubrūhaye
8 Such insight is one's strength.
# Ajj'eva kiccam-ātappaṁ
9 Ardently doing one's task today,
# ko jaññā maraṇaṁ suve
10 Tomorrow, who knows, death may come;
# Na hi no saṅgaran-tena
11 Facing the mighty hordes of death,
# mahā-senena maccunā
12 Indeed one cannot strike a deal.
# Evaṁ vihārim-ātāpiṁ
13 To dwell with energy aroused
# aho-rattam-atanditaṁ
14 Thus for a night of non-decline,
# Taṁ ve bhadd'eka-ratto'ti
15 That is a 'night of shining prosperity.'
# santo ācikkhate muni
16 So it was taught by the Peaceful Sage.
