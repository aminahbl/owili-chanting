[chant:0062 angulimala-paritta]
# Yato'haṁ bhagini ariyāya jātiyā jāto
1 Sister, from the time I was born in a Noble birth
# Nābhijānāmi sañcicca pāṇaṁ jīvitā voropetā
2 I am not aware of having intentionally deprived a being of life,
# Tena saccena sotthi te hotu sotthi gabbhassa
3 through this truth may you be safe, and may your foetus be safe!
