[chant:0051 mangala-sutta]
# Evam-me sutaṁ: ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā,
1 {Thus have I heard that the Blessed One}
# sāvatthiyaṁ viharati,
2 Was staying at Sāvatthī,
# jeta-vane
3 Residing at the Jeta's Grove
# anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme.
4 In Anāthapiṇḍika's Park.
# Atha kho aññatarā devatā abhikkantāya rattiyā abhikkanta-vaṇṇā
5 Then in the dark of the night, a radiant deva
# kevala-kappaṁ jetavanaṁ obhāsetvā,
6 Illuminated all Jeta's Grove.
# yena bhagavā ten'upasaṅkami. Upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṁ abhivādetvā ekam-antaṁ aṭṭhāsi.
7 She bowed down low before the Blessed One
# Ekam-antaṁ ṭhitā kho sā devatā bhagavantaṁ gāthāya ajjhabhāsi:
8 Then standing to one side she said:
# Bahū devā manussā ca,
9 'Devas are concerned for happiness
# Maṅgalāni acintayuṁ;
10 And ever long for peace.
# Ākaṅkhamānā sotthānaṁ,
11 The same is true for humankind.
# Brūhi maṅgalam-uttamaṁ.
12 What then are the highest blessings?'
# Asevanā ca bālānaṁ
13 'Avoiding those of foolish ways,
# Paṇḍitānañ-ca sevanā
14 Associating with the wise,
# Pūjā ca pūjanīyānaṁ
15 And honouring those worthy of honour.
# Etam maṅgalam-uttamaṁ
16 These are the highest blessings.
# Paṭirūpa-desa-vāso ca
17 'Living in places of suitable kinds,
# Pubbe ca kata-puññatā
18 With the fruits of past good deeds
# Atta-sammā-paṇidhi ca
19 And guided by the rightful way.
# Etam maṅgalam-uttamaṁ
20 These are the highest blessings.
# Bāhu-saccañ-ca sippañ-ca,
21 'Accomplished in learning and craftsman's skills,
# Vinayo ca susikkhito
22 With discipline, highly trained,
# Subhāsitā ca yā vācā
23 And speech that is true and pleasant to hear.
# Etam maṅgalam-uttamaṁ
24 These are the highest blessings.
# Mātā-pitu-upaṭṭhānaṁ
25 'Providing for mother and father's support
# Putta-dārassa saṅgaho
26 And cherishing family,
# Anākulā ca kammantā
27 And ways of work that harm no being,
# Etam maṅgalam-uttamaṁ
28 These are the highest blessings.
# Dānañ-ca dhamma-cariyā ca
29 'Generosity and a righteous life,
# Ñātakānañ-ca saṅgaho
30 Offering help to relatives and kin,
# Anavajjāni kammāni
31 And acting in ways that leave no blame.
# Etam maṅgalam-uttamaṁ
32 These are the highest blessings.
# Āratī viratī pāpā
33 'Steadfast in restraint, and shunning evil ways,
# Majja-pānā ca saññamo
34 Avoiding intoxicants that dull the mind,
# Appamādo ca dhammesu
35 And heedfulness in all things that arise.
# Etam maṅgalam-uttamaṁ
36 These are the highest blessings.
# Gāravo ca nivāto ca
37 'Respectfulness and being of humble ways,
# Santuṭṭhī ca kataññutā
38 Contentment and gratitude,
# Kālena dhammassavanaṁ
39 And hearing the Dhamma frequently taught.
# Etam maṅgalam-uttamaṁ
40 These are the highest blessings.
# Khantī ca sovacassatā
41 'Patience and willingness to accept one's faults,
# Samaṇānañ-ca dassanaṁ
42 Seeing venerated seekers of the truth,
# Kālena dhamma-sākacchā
43 And sharing often the words of Dhamma.
# Etam maṅgalam-uttamaṁ
44 These are the highest blessings.
# Tapo ca brahma-cariyañ-ca
45 'Ardent, committed to the Holy Life,
# Ariya-saccāna-dassanaṁ
46 Seeing for oneself the Noble Truths
# Nibbāna-sacchikiriyā ca
47 And the realization of Nibbāna.
# Etam maṅgalam-uttamaṁ
48 These are the highest blessings.
# Phuṭṭhassa loka-dhammehi
49 'Although in contact with the world,
# Cittaṁ yassa na kampati
50 Unshaken the mind remains
# Asokaṁ virajaṁ khemaṁ
51 Beyond all sorrow, spotless, secure.
# Etam maṅgalam-uttamaṁ
52 These are the highest blessings.
# Etādisāni katvāna
53 'They who live by following this path
# Sabbattham-aparājitā
54 Know victory wherever they go,
# Sabbattha sotthiṁ gacchanti
55 And every place for them is safe.
# Tan-tesaṁ maṅgalam-uttaman'ti
56 These are the highest blessings.'
