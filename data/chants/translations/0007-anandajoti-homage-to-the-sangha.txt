[chant:0007 homage-to-the-sangha]
# Yo so supaṭipanno bhagavato sāvakasaṅgho
1 That which is the Fortunate One’s Saṅgha of disciples are good in their practice,
# Ujupaṭipanno bhagavato sāvakasaṅgho
2 the Fortunate One’s Saṅgha of disciples are upright in their practice,
# Ñāyapaṭipanno bhagavato sāvakasaṅgho
3 the Fortunate One’s Saṅgha of disciples are systematic in their practice,
# Sāmīcipaṭipanno bhagavato sāvakasaṅgho
4 the Fortunate One’s Saṅgha of disciples are correct in their practice,
# Yadidaṁ cattāri purisayugāni aṭṭha purisapuggalā
5 that is to say, the four pairs of persons, the eight individual persons,
# Esa bhagavato sāvakasaṅgho
6 this is the Fortunate One’s Saṅgha of disciples,
# Āhuneyyo,
7 they are worthy of offerings,
# Pāhuneyyo,
8 of hospitality,
# Dakkhiṇeyyo,
9 of gifts,
# Añjali-karaṇīyo
10 and of reverential salutation,
# Anuttaraṁ puññakkhettaṁ lokassa
11 they are an unsurpassed field of merit for the world.
# Tam-ahaṁ saṅghaṁ abhipūjayāmi tam-ahaṁ saṅghaṁ sirasā namāmi
12 I worship that Saṅgha, I bow my head to that Saṅgha.
