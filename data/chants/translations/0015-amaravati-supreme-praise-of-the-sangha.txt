[chant:0015 supreme-praise-of-the-sangha]
# Saddhammajo supaṭipatti-guṇādiyutto
1 Born of the Dhamma, that Saṅgha which has practised well,
# Yo'ṭṭhabbidho ariyapuggala-saṅgha-seṭṭho
2 The field of the Saṅgha formed of eight kinds of noble beings,
# Sīlādidhamma-pavarāsaya-kāya-citto
3 Guided in body and mind by excellent morality and virtue.
# Vandām'ahaṁ tam-ariyāna-gaṇaṁ susuddhaṁ
4 I revere that assembly of noble beings perfected in purity.
# Saṅgho yo sabba-pāṇīnaṁ saraṇaṁ khemam-uttamaṁ
5 The Saṅgha, which is the supreme, secure refuge of all beings —
# Tatiyānussatiṭṭhānaṁ vandāmi taṁ siren'ahaṁ
6 As the Third Object of Recollection, I venerate it with bowed head.
# Saṅghass'āhasmi dāso va saṅgho me sāmi-kissaro
7 I am indeed the Saṅgha's servant, the Saṅgha is my Lord and Guide.
# Saṅgho dukkhassa ghātā ca vidhātā ca hitassa me
8 The Saṅgha is sorrow's destroyer and it bestows blessings on me.
# Saṅghass'āhaṁ niyyādemi sarīrañ-jīvitañ-cidaṁ
9 To the Saṅgha I dedicate this body and life,
# Vandanto'haṁ carissāmi saṅghassopaṭipannataṁ
10 And in devotion I will walk the well-practised way of the Saṅgha.
# Natthi me saraṇaṁ aññaṁ saṅgho me saraṇaṁ varaṁ
11 For me there is no other refuge, the Saṅgha is my excellent refuge.
# Etena sacca-vajjena vaḍḍheyyaṁ satthu-sāsane
12 By the utterance of this Truth, may I grow in the Master's Way.
# Saṅghaṁ me vandamānena yaṁ puññaṁ pasutaṁ idha
13 By my devotion to the Saṅgha, and the blessing of this practice —
# Sabbepi antarāyā me māhesuṁ tassa tejasā
14 By its power, may all obstacles be overcome.
# Kāyena vācāya va cetasā vā
16 By body, speech, or mind,
# Saṅghe kukammaṁ pakataṁ mayā yaṁ
17 For whatever wrong action I have committed towards the Saṅgha,
# Saṅgho paṭiggaṇhātu accayantaṁ
18 May my acknowledgement of fault be accepted,
# Kālantare saṁvarituṁ va saṅghe
19 That in future there may be restraint regarding the Saṅgha.
