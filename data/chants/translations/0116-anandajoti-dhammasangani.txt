[chant:0116 dhammasangani]
# Kusalā dhammā, akusalā dhammā, abyākatā dhammā.
1 Wholesome thoughts, unwholesome thoughts, thoughts without consequences.
# Katame dhammā kusalā.
2 What are wholesome thoughts?
# Yasmiṁ samaye kāmāvacaraṁ kusalaṁ cittaṁ uppannaṁ hoti,
3 When a wholesome thought in the sensuous worlds has arisen,
# somanassa-sahagataṁ ñāṇa-sampayuttaṁ,
4 connected with well-being, joined with knowledge,
# rūpārammaṇaṁ vā saddārammaṇaṁ vā gandhārammaṇaṁ vā rasārammaṇaṁ
5 taking form as object, or sound as object, or smell as object, or taste as object,
# vā phoṭṭhabbārammaṇaṁ vā dhammārammaṇaṁ vā,
6 or touch as object, or thought as object,
# yaṁ yaṁ vā panārabbha, tasmiṁ samaye phasso hoti, avikkhepo hoti,
7 or whatever it begins with, at that time there is contact... balance.
# ye vā pana tasmiṁ samaye aññe pi atthi paṭicca-samuppannā arūpino dhammā,
8 At that time, these or other formless thoughts are conditionally originated.
# ime dhammā kusalā.
9 These are wholesome thoughts.
