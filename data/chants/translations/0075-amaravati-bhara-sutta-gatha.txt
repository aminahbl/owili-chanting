[chant:0075 bhara-sutta-gatha]
# Bhārā have pañcakkhandhā
1 The five aggregates indeed are burdens,
# bhāra-hāro ca puggalo
2 The beast of burden though is man.
# Bhār'ādānaṁ dukkhaṁ loke
3 In this world to take up burdens is dukkha.
# bhāra-nikkhepanaṁ sukhaṁ
4 Putting them down brings happiness.
# Nikkhipitvā garuṁ bhāraṁ
5 A heavy burden cast away,
# aññaṁ bhāraṁ anādiya
6 Not taking on another load,
# Samūlaṁ taṇhaṁ abbuyha
7 With craving pulled out from the root,
# nicchāto parinibbuto
8 Desires stilled, one is released.
