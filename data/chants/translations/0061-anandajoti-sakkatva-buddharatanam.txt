[chant:0061 sakkatva-buddharatanam]
# Sakkatvā buddharatanaṁ
1 Having honoured the Buddha treasure,
# osadhaṁ uttamaṁ varaṁ
2 the best and supreme medicine,
# Hitaṁ devamanussānaṁ
3 of benefit to gods and men,
# buddhatejena sotthinā
4 by the blessed power of the Buddha
# Nassantupaddavā sabbe
5 may all adversities perish,
# dukkhā vūpasamentu te
6 and all suffering come to an end for you!
# Sakkatvā dhammaratanaṁ
7 Having honoured the Dhamma treasure,
# osadhaṁ uttamaṁ varaṁ
8 the best and supreme medicine,
# Pariḷāhūpasamanaṁ
9 which brings fevers to an end,
# dhammatejena sotthinā
10 by the blessed power of the Dhamma
# Nassantupaddavā sabbe
11 may all adversities perish,
# bhayā vūpasamentu te
12 and all fear come to an end for you!
# Sakkatvā saṅgharatanaṁ
13 Having honoured the Saṅgha treasure,
# osadhaṁ uttamaṁ varaṁ
14 the best and supreme medicine,
# Āhuneyyaṁ pāhuneyyaṁ
15 worthy of gifts and hospitality,
# saṅghatejena sotthinā
16 by the blessed power of the Saṅgha
# Nassantupaddavā sabbe
17 may all adversities perish,
# rogā vūpasamentu te
18 and all disease come to an end for you!
