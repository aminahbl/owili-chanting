[chant:0014 recollection-of-the-sangha]
# Supaṭipanno bhagavato sāvakasaṅgho
1 They are the Blessed One's disciples, who have practised well,
# Ujupaṭipanno bhagavato sāvakasaṅgho
2 Who have practised directly,
# Ñāyapaṭipanno bhagavato sāvakasaṅgho
3 Who have practised insightfully,
# Sāmīcipaṭipanno bhagavato sāvakasaṅgho
4 Those who practise with integrity —
# Yadidaṁ cattāri purisayugāni aṭṭha purisapuggalā
5 That is the four pairs, the eight kinds of noble beings —
# Esa bhagavato sāvakasaṅgho
6 These are the Blessed One's disciples.
# Āhuneyyo pāhuneyyo dakkhiṇeyyo añjali-karaṇīyo
7 Such ones are worthy of gifts, worthy of hospitality, worthy of offerings, worthy of respect;
# Anuttaraṁ puññakkhettaṁ lokassā'ti
8 They give occasion for incomparable goodness to arise in the world.
