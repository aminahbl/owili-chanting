[chant:0009 closing-homage]
# {Arahaṁ} sammāsambuddho bhagavā
1 {The Lord,} the Perfectly Enlightened and Blessed One —
# Buddhaṁ bhagavantaṁ abhivādemi
2 I render homage to the Buddha, the Blessed One.
# {Svākkhāto} bhagavatā dhammo
3 {The Teaching,} so completely explained by him —
# Dhammaṁ namassāmi
4 I bow to the Dhamma.
# {Supaṭipanno} bhagavato sāvakasaṅgho
5 {The Blessed One's disciples,} who have practised well —
# Saṅghaṁ namāmi
6 I bow to the Saṅgha.
