[chant:0030 ten-subjects-for-frequent-recollection]
# {Dasa ime bhikkhave} dhammā pabbajitena abhiṇhaṁ paccavekkhitabbā, katame dasa
1 {“There are these ten things, monks,} that one who has gone forth should frequently reflect on. What are the ten?
# Vevaṇṇiyamhi ajjhupagato'ti
2 “I have become one who has no (distinctive) appearance”,
# pabbajitena abhiṇhaṁ paccavekkhitabbaṁ
3 one who has gone forth should frequently reflect on this.
# Parapaṭibaddhā me jīvikā'ti
4 “I am bound to others for my livelihood”,
# pabbajitena abhiṇhaṁ paccavekkhitabbaṁ
5 one who has gone forth should frequently reflect on this.
# Añño me ākappo karaṇīyo'ti
6 “I should comport myself differently”,
# pabbajitena abhiṇhaṁ paccavekkhitabbaṁ
7 one who has gone forth should frequently reflect on this.
# Kacci nu kho me attā sīlato na upavadatī'ti
8 “Can I myself find no fault with my virtue?”
# pabbajitena abhiṇhaṁ paccavekkhitabbaṁ
9 one who has gone forth should frequently reflect on this.
# Kacci nu kho maṁ anuvicca viññū sabrahmacārī sīlato na upavadantī'ti
10 “Will my wise companions in the spiritual life, after testing me, find no fault with my virtue?”,
# pabbajitena abhiṇhaṁ paccavekkhitabbaṁ
11 one who has gone forth should frequently reflect on this.
# Sabbehi me piyehi manāpehi nānābhāvo vinābhāvo'ti
12 “There is alteration in, and separation from, all that is dear and appealing to me”,
# pabbajitena abhiṇhaṁ paccavekkhitabbaṁ
13 one who has gone forth should frequently reflect on this.
# Kammassakomhi kammadāyādo kammayoni kammabandhu kammapaṭisaraṇo,
14 “It is actions that I own, it is actions that I am heir to, it is actions that I am born from, actions are my kinsfolk, actions are my refuge -
# yaṁ kammaṁ karissāmi, kalyāṇaṁ vā pāpakaṁ vā, tassa dāyādo bhavissāmī'ti
15 whatever actions I perform, whether good or bad, to that I will be the heir”,
# pabbajitena abhiṇhaṁ paccavekkhitabbaṁ
16 one who has gone forth should frequently reflect on this.
# 'Kathambhūtassa me rattindivā vītipatantī'ti
17 “In what way do the nights and days pass for me?”
# pabbajitena abhiṇhaṁ paccavekkhitabbaṁ
18 one who has gone forth should frequently reflect on this.
# Kacci nu kho'haṁ suññāgāre abhiramāmī'ti
19 “Do I delight in empty places?”
# pabbajitena abhiṇhaṁ paccavekkhitabbaṁ
20 one who has gone forth should frequently reflect on this.
# Atthi nu kho me uttari-manussa-dhammā alamariya-ñāṇa-dassana-viseso adhigato,
21 “Has a state beyond (ordinary) human beings - the distinction of what is truly noble knowledge and seeing - been attained by me?
# so'haṁ pacchime kāle sabrahmacārīhi puṭṭho na maṅku bhavissāmī'ti
22 Will I at the end, when questioned by my companions in the spiritual life, not be embarrassed?”
# pabbajitena abhiṇhaṁ paccavekkhitabbaṁ
23 one who has gone forth should frequently reflect on this.
# Ime kho bhikkhave dasa dhammā pabbajitena abhiṇhaṁ paccavekkhitabbā'ti
24 These are the ten things, monks, that one who has gone forth should frequently reflect on.”
