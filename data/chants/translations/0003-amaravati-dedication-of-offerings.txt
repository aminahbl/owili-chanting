[chant:0003 dedication-of-offerings]
# {Yo so} bhagavā arahaṁ sammāsambuddho
1 {To the Blessed One,} the Lord, who fully attained perfect enlightenment,
# Svākkhāto yena bhagavatā dhammo
2 To the Teaching, which he expounded so well,
# Supaṭipanno yassa bhagavato sāvakasaṅgho
3 And to the Blessed One's disciples who have practised well,
# Tam-mayaṁ bhagavantaṁ sadhammaṁ sasaṅghaṁ
4 To these — the Buddha, the Dhamma, and the Saṅgha —
# Imehi sakkārehi yathārahaṁ āropitehi abhipūjayāma
5 We render with offerings our rightful homage.
# Sādhu no bhante bhagavā sucira-parinibbutopi
6 It is well for us that the Blessed One, having attained liberation,
# Pacchimā-janatānukampa-mānasā
7 Still had compassion for later generations.
# Ime sakkāre duggata-paṇṇākāra-bhūte paṭiggaṇhātu
8 May these simple offerings be accepted
# Amhākaṁ dīgharattaṁ hitāya sukhāya
9 For our long-lasting benefit and for the happiness it gives us.
# Arahaṁ sammāsambuddho bhagavā
10 The Lord, the Perfectly Enlightened and Blessed One —
# Buddhaṁ bhagavantaṁ abhivādemi
11 I render homage to the Buddha, the Blessed One.
# {Svākkhāto} bhagavatā dhammo
12 {The Teaching,} so completely explained by him —
# Dhammaṁ namassāmi
13 I bow to the Dhamma.
# {Supaṭipanno} bhagavato sāvakasaṅgho
14 {The Blessed One's disciples,} who have practised well —
# Saṅghaṁ namāmi
15 I bow to the Saṅgha.
