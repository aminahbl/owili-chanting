[chant:0045 reflection-on-impermanence]
# Sabbe saṅkhārā aniccā
1 All conditioned things are impermanent;
# Sabbe saṅkhārā dukkhā
2 All conditioned things are dukkha;
# Sabbe dhammā anattā
3 Everything is void of self.
# Addhuvaṁ jīvitaṁ
4 Life is not for sure;
# Dhuvaṁ maraṇaṁ
5 Death is for sure;
# Avassaṁ mayā maritabbaṁ
6 It is inevitable that I'll die;
# Maraṇa-pariyosānaṁ me jīvitaṁ
7 Death is the culmination of my life;
# Jīvitaṁ me aniyataṁ
8 My life is uncertain;
# Maraṇaṁ me niyataṁ
9 My death is certain.
# Vata
10 Indeed,
# Ayaṁ kāyo
11 This body
# Aciraṁ
12 Will soon
# Apeta-viññāṇo
13 Be void of consciousness
# Chuddho
14 And cast away.
# Adhisessati
15 It will lie
# Paṭhaviṁ
16 On the ground
# Kaliṅgaraṁ iva
17 Just like a rotten log,
# Niratthaṁ
18 Completely void of use.
# Aniccā vata saṅkhārā
19 Truly conditioned things cannot last,
# Uppāda-vaya-dhammino
20 Their nature is to rise and fall,
# Uppajjitvā nirujjhanti
21 Having arisen things must cease,
# Tesaṁ vūpasamo sukho
22 Their stilling is true happiness.
