[chant:0010 recollection-of-the-buddha]
# Taṁ kho pana bhagavantaṁ evaṁ kalyāṇo kittisaddo abbhuggato
1 This good report has gone round about the Fortunate One,
# Itipi so bhagavā arahaṁ sammāsambuddho
2 Such is he, the Fortunate One, the Worthy One, the Perfect Sambuddha,
# Vijjācaraṇa-sampanno sugato lokavidū
3 the one endowed with understanding and good conduct, the Happy One, the one who understands the worlds,
# Anuttaro purisadamma-sārathi satthā deva-manussānaṁ buddho bhagavā'ti
4 the unsurpassed guide for those people who need taming, the Teacher of gods and men, the Buddha, the Fortunate One.
