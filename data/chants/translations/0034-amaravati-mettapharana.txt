[chant:0034 mettapharana]
# Ahaṁ sukhito homi
1 May I abide in well-being,
# Niddukkho homi,
2 In freedom from affliction,
# Avero homi,
3 In freedom from hostility,
# Abyāpajjho homi,
4 In freedom from ill-will,
# Anīgho homi,
5 In freedom from anxiety,
# Sukhī attānaṁ pariharāmi
6 And may I maintain well-being in myself.
# Sabbe sattā sukhitā hontu,
7 May everyone abide in well-being,
# Sabbe sattā averā hontu,
8 In freedom from hostility,
# Sabbe sattā abyāpajjhā hontu,
9 In freedom from ill-will,
# Sabbe sattā anīghā hontu,
10 In freedom from anxiety, and may they
# Sabbe sattā sukhī attānaṁ pariharantu
11 Maintain well-being in themselves.
# Sabbe sattā sabbadukkhā pamuccantu
12 May all beings be released from all suffering.
# Sabbe sattā laddha-sampattito mā vigacchantu
13 And may they not be parted from the good fortune they have attained.
# Sabbe sattā kammassakā kammadāyādā kammayonī kammabandhū kammapaṭisaraṇā,
14 When they act upon intention, All beings are the owners of their action and inherit its results. Their future is born from such action, companion to such action, And its results will be their home.
# Yaṁ kammaṁ karissanti,
15 All actions with intention,
# Kalyāṇaṁ vā pāpakaṁ vā,
16 Be they skilful or harmful —
# Tassa dāyādā bhavissanti
17 Of such acts they will be the heirs.
