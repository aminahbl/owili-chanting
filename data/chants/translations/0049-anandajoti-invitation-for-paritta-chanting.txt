[chant:0049 invitation-for-paritta-chanting]
# Vipatti-paṭibāhāya sabba-sampatti-siddhiyā
1 For the warding off of misfortune, for the accomplishment of all good fortune,
# Sabbadukkha-vināsāya
2 for the destruction of all suffering:
# Parittaṁ brūtha maṅgalaṁ
3 please chant the auspicious safeguard.
# Vipatti-paṭibāhāya sabba-sampatti-siddhiyā
4 For the warding off of misfortune, for the accomplishment of all good fortune,
# Sabbabhaya-vināsāya
5 for the destruction of all fear:
# Parittaṁ brūtha maṅgalaṁ
6 please chant the auspicious safeguard.
# Vipatti-paṭibāhāya sabba-sampatti-siddhiyā
7 For the warding off of misfortune, for the accomplishment of all good fortune,
# Sabbaroga-vināsāya
8 for the destruction of all disease:
# Parittaṁ brūtha maṅgalaṁ
9 please chant the auspicious safeguard.
