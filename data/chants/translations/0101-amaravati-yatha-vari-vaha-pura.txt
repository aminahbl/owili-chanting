[chant:0101 yatha-vari-vaha-pura]
# Yathā vāri-vahā pūrā paripūrenti sāgaraṁ
1 Just as rivers full of water entirely fill up the sea
# Evam-eva ito dinnaṁ petānaṁ upakappati
2 So will what's here been given bring blessings to departed spirits.
# Icchitaṁ patthitaṁ tumhaṁ
3 May all your hopes and all your longings
# Khippam-eva samijjhatu
4 Come true in no long time.
# Sabbe pūrentu saṅkappā
5 May all your wishes be fulfilled
# Cando paṇṇaraso yathā
6 Like on the fifteenth day the moon
# Maṇi jotiraso yathā
7 Or like a bright and shining gem.
# Sabb'ītiyo vivajjantu
8 May all misfortunes be avoided,
# Sabba-rogo vinassatu
9 May all illness be dispelled,
# Mā te bhavatv-antarāyo
10 May you never meet with dangers,
# Sukhī dīgh'āyuko bhava
11 May you be happy and live long.
# Abhivādana-sīlissa
12 For those who are respectful,
# Niccaṁ vuḍḍhāpacāyino
13 Who always honour the elders,
# Cattāro dhammā vaḍḍhanti
14 Four are the qualities which will increase:
# Āyu vaṇṇo sukhaṁ balaṁ
15 Life, beauty, happiness and strength.
# Bhavatu sabba-maṅgalaṁ
16 May every blessing come to be
# Rakkhantu sabba-devatā
17 And all good spirits guard you well.
# Sabba-buddhānubhāvena
18 Through the power of all Buddhas
# Sadā sotthī bhavantu te
19 May you always be at ease.
# Bhavatu sabba-maṅgalaṁ
20 May every blessing come to be
# Rakkhantu sabba-devatā
21 And all good spirits guard you well.
# Sabba-dhammānubhāvena
22 Through the power of all Dhammas
# Sadā sotthī bhavantu te
23 May you always be at ease.
# Bhavatu sabba-maṅgalaṁ
24 May every blessing come to be
# Rakkhantu sabba-devatā
25 And all good spirits guard you well.
# Sabba-saṅghānubhāvena
26 Through the power of all Saṅghas
# Sadā sotthī bhavantu te
27 May you always be at ease.
