[chant:0119 puggalapannatti]
# Cha paññattiyo khandhapaññatti, āyatanapaññatti, dhātupaññatti, saccapaññatti,
1 There are six designations: the designation of the components, the designation of the sense-spheres, the designation of the elements, the designation of the truths,
# indriyapaññatti, puggalapaññattī'ti.
2 the designation of the faculties, the designation of the persons.
# Kittāvatā puggalānaṁ puggalapaññatti.
3 In what way is there a designation of the person regarding persons?
# Samayavimutto, asamayavimutto,
4 One free occasionally, one free always,
# kuppadhammo, akuppadhammo,
5 one unsteady, one steady,
# parihānadhammo, aparihānadhammo,
6 one liable to fall, one not liable to fall,
# cetanābhabbo, anurakkhaṇābhabbo,
7 one able in intention, one able in guarding,
# puthujjano, gotrabhū,
8 the worldling, the lineage-holder,
# bhayūparato, abhayūparato,
9 one abstaining through fear, one not abstaining through fear,
# bhabbāgamano, abhabbāgamano,
10 one able to arrive, one unable to arrive,
# niyato, aniyato,
11 one determined, one undetermined,
# paṭipannako, phaleṭhito,
12 one path-attained, one established in fruition...
# arahā, arahattāya paṭipanno.
13 the Worthy One, the one on the path to Worthiness.
