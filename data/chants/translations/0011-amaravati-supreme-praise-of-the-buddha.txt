[chant:0011 supreme-praise-of-the-buddha]
# Buddh'vārahanta-varatādiguṇābhiyutto
1 The Buddha, the truly worthy one, endowed with such excellent qualities,
# Suddhābhiñāṇa-karuṇāhi samāgatatto
2 Whose being is composed of purity, transcendental wisdom, and compassion,
# Bodhesi yo sujanataṁ kamalaṁ va sūro
3 Who has enlightened the wise like the sun awakening the lotus —
# Vandām'ahaṁ tam-araṇaṁ sirasā jinendaṁ
4 I bow my head to that peaceful chief of conquerors.
# Buddho yo sabba-pāṇīnaṁ saraṇaṁ khemam-uttamaṁ
5 The Buddha, who is the safe, secure refuge of all beings —
# Paṭhamānussatiṭṭhānaṁ vandāmi taṁ siren'ahaṁ
6 As the First Object of Recollection, I venerate him with bowed head.
# Buddhassāh'asmi dāso va buddho me sāmi-kissaro
7 I am indeed the Buddha's servant, the Buddha is my Lord and Guide.
# Buddho dukkhassa ghātā ca vidhātā ca hitassa me
8 The Buddha is sorrow's destroyer, who bestows blessings on me.
# Buddhass'āhaṁ niyyādemi sarīrañ-jīvitañ-cidaṁ
9 To the Buddha I dedicate this body and life,
# Vandanto'haṁ carissāmi buddhass'eva subodhitaṁ
10 And in devotion I will walk the Buddha's Path of Awakening.
# Natthi me saraṇaṁ aññaṁ buddho me saraṇaṁ varaṁ
11 For me there is no other refuge, the Buddha is my excellent refuge.
# Etena sacca-vajjena vaḍḍheyyaṁ satthu-sāsane
12 By the utterance of this Truth, may I grow in the Master's Way.
# Buddhaṁ me vandamānena yaṁ puññaṁ pasutaṁ idha
13 By my devotion to the Buddha, and the blessing of this practice —
# Sabbepi antarāyā me māhesuṁ tassa tejasā
14 By its power, may all obstacles be overcome.
# Kāyena vācāya va cetasā vā
16 By body, speech, or mind,
# Buddhe kukammaṁ pakataṁ mayā yaṁ
17 For whatever wrong action I have committed towards the Buddha,
# Buddho paṭiggaṇhātu accayantaṁ
18 May my acknowledgement of fault be accepted,
# Kālantare saṁvarituṁ va buddhe
19 That in future there may be restraint regarding the Buddha.
