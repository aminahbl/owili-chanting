[chant:0031 caturappamanna-obhasana]
# {Mettā-sahagatena} cetasā ekaṁ disaṁ pharitvā viharati
1 {I will abide} pervading one quarter with a heart imbued with loving-kindness;
# Tathā dutiyaṁ tathā tatiyaṁ tathā catutthaṁ
2 Likewise the second, likewise the third, likewise the fourth;
# Iti uddhamadho tiriyaṁ sabbadhi sabbattatāya
3 So above and below, around and everywhere; and to all as to myself.
# Sabbāvantaṁ lokaṁ mettā-sahagatena cetasā
4 I will abide pervading the all-encompassing world with a heart imbued with loving-kindness;
# Vipulena mahaggatena appamāṇena averena abyāpajjhena pharitvā viharati
5 abundant, exalted, immeasurable, without hostility, and without ill-will.
# Karuṇā-sahagatena cetasā ekaṁ disaṁ pharitvā viharati
6 I will abide pervading one quarter with a heart imbued with compassion;
# Tathā dutiyaṁ tathā tatiyaṁ tathā catutthaṁ
7 Likewise the second, likewise the third, likewise the fourth;
# Iti uddhamadho tiriyaṁ sabbadhi sabbattatāya
8 So above and below, around and everywhere; and to all as to myself.
# Sabbāvantaṁ lokaṁ karuṇā-sahagatena cetasā
9 I will abide pervading the all-encompassing world with a heart imbued with compassion;
# Vipulena mahaggatena appamāṇena averena abyāpajjhena pharitvā viharati
10 abundant, exalted, immeasurable, without hostility, and without ill-will.
# Muditā-sahagatena cetasā ekaṁ disaṁ pharitvā viharati
11 I will abide pervading one quarter with a heart imbued with gladness;
# Tathā dutiyaṁ tathā tatiyaṁ tathā catutthaṁ
12 Likewise the second, likewise the third, likewise the fourth;
# Iti uddhamadho tiriyaṁ sabbadhi sabbattatāya
13 So above and below, around and everywhere; and to all as to myself.
# Sabbāvantaṁ lokaṁ muditā-sahagatena cetasā
14 I will abide pervading the all-encompassing world with a heart imbued with gladness;
# Vipulena mahaggatena appamāṇena averena abyāpajjhena pharitvā viharati
15 abundant, exalted, immeasurable, without hostility, and without ill-will.
# Upekkhā-sahagatena cetasā ekaṁ disaṁ pharitvā viharati
16 I will abide pervading one quarter with a heart imbued with equanimity;
# Tathā dutiyaṁ tathā tatiyaṁ tathā catutthaṁ
17 Likewise the second, likewise the third, likewise the fourth;
# Iti uddhamadho tiriyaṁ sabbadhi sabbattatāya
18 So above and below, around and everywhere; and to all as to myself.
# Sabbāvantaṁ lokaṁ upekkhā-sahagatena cetasā
19 I will abide pervading the all-encompassing world with a heart imbued with equanimity;
# Vipulena mahaggatena appamāṇena averena abyāpajjhena pharitvā viharatī'ti
20 abundant, exalted, immeasurable, without hostility, and without ill-will.
