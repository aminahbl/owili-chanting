[chant:0015 supreme-praise-of-the-sangha]
# Saddhammajo supaṭipatti-guṇādiyutto
1 Born of the Dhamma, endowed with the virtue of good practice,
# Yo'ṭṭhabbidho ariyapuggala-saṅgha-seṭṭho
2 the best of Saṅghas has eight divisions of Noble persons,
# Sīlādidhamma-pavarāsaya-kāya-citto
3 my body and mind are inclined to the noble Dhamma, beginning with virtue,
# Vandām'ahaṁ tam-ariyāna-gaṇaṁ susuddhaṁ
4 I worship that pure community of Noble Ones.
# Saṅgho yo sabba-pāṇīnaṁ saraṇaṁ khemam-uttamaṁ
5 The Saṅgha, which is the supreme refuge for all living beings,
# Tatiyānussatiṭṭhānaṁ vandāmi taṁ siren'ahaṁ
6 I recall third in this place, I worship it with my head,
# Saṅghass'āhasmi dāso va saṅgho me sāmi-kissaro
7 I am the servant of the Saṅgha, the Saṅgha is my lord and master.
# Saṅgho dukkhassa ghātā ca vidhātā ca hitassa me
8 the Saṅgha is a destroyer of suffering, a provider (of solace) and it benefits me.
# Saṅghass'āhaṁ niyyādemi sarīrañ-jīvitañ-cidaṁ
9 I dedicate this body and this life to the Saṅgha.
# Vandanto'haṁ carissāmi saṅghassopaṭipannataṁ
10 I will live worshipping the Saṅgha’s good practice.
# Natthi me saraṇaṁ aññaṁ saṅgho me saraṇaṁ varaṁ
11 For me there is no other refuge, for me the Saṅgha is the best refuge!
# Etena sacca-vajjena vaḍḍheyyaṁ satthu-sāsane
12 By this declaration of the truth may the Teacher’s dispensation prosper.
# Saṅghaṁ me vandamānena yaṁ puññaṁ pasutaṁ idha
13 Through my worship of the Saṅgha and whatever merit is found here,
# Sabbepi antarāyā me māhesuṁ tassa tejasā
14 through the power of that, may there be no dangers for me.
# Kāyena vācāya va cetasā vā
16 By body, by speech or by mind,
# Saṅghe kukammaṁ pakataṁ mayā yaṁ
17 whatever bad deeds were done by me regarding the Saṅgha,
# Saṅgho paṭiggaṇhātu accayantaṁ
18 may the Saṅgha accept my confession,
# Kālantare saṁvarituṁ va saṅghe
19 for my restraint in the future regarding the Saṅgha.
