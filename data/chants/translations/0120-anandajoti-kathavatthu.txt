[chant:0120 kathavatthu]
# Puggalo upalabbhati saccikaṭṭha-paramatthenā'ti.
1 Is a person found in a real and ultimate way?
# Āmantā.
2 Yes.
# Yo saccikaṭṭho paramattho,
3 That which is real and ultimate,
# tato so puggalo upalabbhati saccikaṭṭha-paramatthenā'ti.
4 is this person found in (the same) real and ultimate way?
# Na h'evaṁ vattabbe.
5 No, that cannot be said.
# Ājānāhi niggahaṁ.
6 Acknowledge the refutation.
# Hañci puggalo upalabbhati saccikaṭṭha-paramatthena, tena vata re vattabbe.
7 If a person is found in a real and ultimate way, then you surely should have said:
# Yo saccikaṭṭho paramattho,
8 That which is real and ultimate,
# tato so puggalo upalabbhati saccikaṭṭha-paramatthenā'ti micchā.
9 this person is found in (the same) real and ultimate way. (Your view is) wrong.
