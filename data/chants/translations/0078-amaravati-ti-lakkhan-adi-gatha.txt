[chant:0078 ti-lakkhan-adi-gatha]
# Sabbe saṅkhārā aniccā'ti
1 'Impermanent are all conditioned things' —
# yadā paññāya passati
2 When with wisdom this is seen
# Atha nibbindati dukkhe
3 One feels weary of all dukkha;
# esa maggo visuddhiyā
4 This is the path to purity.
# Sabbe saṅkhārā dukkhā'ti
5 'Dukkha are all conditioned things' —
# yadā paññāya passati
6 When with wisdom this is seen
# Atha nibbindati dukkhe
7 One feels weary of all dukkha;
# esa maggo visuddhiyā
8 This is the path to purity.
# Sabbe dhammā anattā'ti
9 'There is no self in anything' —
# yadā paññāya passati
10 When with wisdom this is seen
# Atha nibbindati dukkhe
11 One feels weary of all dukkha;
# esa maggo visuddhiyā
12 This is the path to purity.
# Appakā te manussesu
13 Few amongst humankind
# ye janā pāra-gāmino
14 Are those who go beyond,
# Athāyaṁ itarā pajā
15 Yet there are the many folks
# tīram-evānudhāvati
16 Ever wand'ring on this shore.
# Ye ca kho sammad-akkhāte
17 Wherever Dhamma is well-taught,
# dhamme dhammānuvattino
18 Those who train in line with it
# Te janā pāram-essanti
19 Are the ones who will cross over
# maccu-dheyyaṁ suduttaraṁ
20 The realm of death so hard to flee.
# Kaṇhaṁ dhammaṁ vippahāya
21 Abandoning the darker states,
# sukkaṁ bhāvetha paṇḍito
22 The wise pursue the bright;
# Okā anokam-āgamma
23 From the floods dry land they reach
# viveke yattha dūramaṁ
24 Living withdrawn so hard to do.
# Tatrābhiratim-iccheyya
25 Such rare delight one should desire,
# hitvā kāme akiñcano
26 Sense pleasures cast away, Not having anything.
