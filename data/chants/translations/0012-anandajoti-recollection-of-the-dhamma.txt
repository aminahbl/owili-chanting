[chant:0012 recollection-of-the-dhamma]
# Svākkhāto bhagavatā dhammo
1 The Dhamma has been well-proclaimed by the Fortunate One,
# Sandiṭṭhiko akāliko ehipassiko
2 it is visible, not subject to time, inviting inspection,
# Opanayiko paccattaṁ veditabbo viññūhī'ti
3 onward leading, and can be understood by the wise for themselves.
