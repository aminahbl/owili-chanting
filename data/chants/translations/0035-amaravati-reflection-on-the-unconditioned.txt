[chant:0035 reflection-on-the-unconditioned]
# Atthi bhikkhave ajātaṁ abhūtaṁ akataṁ asaṅkhataṁ
1 There is an Unborn, Unoriginated, Uncreated and Unformed.
# No cetaṁ bhikkhave abhavissa ajātaṁ abhūtaṁ akataṁ asaṅkhataṁ
2 If there was not this Unborn, this Unoriginated, this Uncreated, this Unformed,
# Na yidaṁ jātassa bhūtassa katassa saṅkhatassa nissaraṇaṁ paññāyetha
3 Freedom from the world of the born, the originated, the created, the formed would not be possible.
# Yasmā ca kho bhikkhave atthi ajātaṁ abhūtaṁ akataṁ asaṅkhataṁ
4 But since there is an Unborn, Unoriginated, Uncreated and Unformed,
# Tasmā jātassa bhūtassa katassa saṅkhatassa nissaraṇaṁ paññāyati
5 Therefore is freedom possible from the world of the born, the originated, the created and the formed.
