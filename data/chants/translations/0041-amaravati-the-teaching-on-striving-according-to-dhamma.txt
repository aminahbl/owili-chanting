[chant:0041 the-teaching-on-striving-according-to-dhamma]
# Evaṁ svākkhāto bhikkhave mayā dhammo
1 Bhikkhus, the Dhamma has thus been well expounded by me,
# Uttāno
2 Elucidated,
# Vivaṭo
3 Disclosed,
# Pakāsito
4 Revealed,
# Chinna-pilotiko
5 And stripped of patchwork —
# Alam-eva saddhā-pabbajitena kula-puttena vīriyaṁ ārabhituṁ
6 This is enough for a clansman, who has gone forth out of faith, to arouse his energy thus:
# Kāmaṁ taco ca nahāru ca aṭṭhi ca avasissatu
7 'Willingly let only my skin, sinews and bones remain,
# Sarīre upasussatu maṁsa-lohitaṁ
8 And let the flesh and blood in this body wither away.
# Yaṁ taṁ
9 As long as whatever is to be attained
# Purisa-thāmena
10 By human strength,
# Purisa-vīriyena
11 By human energy,
# Purisa-parakkamena
12 By human effort,
# Pattabbaṁ na taṁ apāpuṇitvā
13 Has not been attained,
# Vīriyassa saṇṭhānaṁ bhavissatī'ti
14 Let not my efforts stand still.'
# Dukkhaṁ bhikkhave kusīto viharati
15 Bhikkhus, the lazy person dwells in suffering,
# Vokiṇṇo pāpakehi akusalehi dhammehi
16 Soiled by evil, unwholesome states
# Mahantañca sadatthaṁ parihāpeti
17 And great is the personal good that he neglects.
# Āraddha-vīriyo ca kho bhikkhave sukhaṁ viharati
18 The energetic person though dwells happily,
# Pavivitto pāpakehi akusalehi dhammehi
19 Well withdrawn from unwholesome states
# Mahantañca sadatthaṁ paripūreti
20 And great is the personal good that he achieves.
# Na bhikkhave hīnena aggassa patti hoti
21 Bhikkhus, it is not by lower means that the supreme is attained
# Aggena ca kho bhikkhave aggassa patti hoti
22 But, bhikkhus, it is by the supreme that the supreme is attained.
# Maṇḍapeyyam-idaṁ bhikkhave brahmacariyaṁ
23 Bhikkhus, this holy life is like the cream of the milk:
# Satthā sammukhī-bhūto
24 The Teacher is present,
# Tasmātiha bhikkhave vīriyaṁ ārabhatha
25 Therefore, bhikkhus, start to arouse your energy
# Appattassa pattiyā
26 For the attainment of the as yet unattained,
# Anadhigatassa adhigamāya
27 For the achievement of the as yet unachieved,
# Asacchikatassa sacchikiriyāya
28 For the realization of the as yet unrealized.
# Evaṁ no ayaṁ amhākaṁ pabbajjā avaṅkatā avañjhā bhavissati
29 Thinking, in such a way: 'Our Going Forth will not be barren
# Saphalā sa-udrayā
30 But will become fruitful and fertile,
# Yesaṁ mayaṁ paribhuñjāma cīvara-piṇḍapāta-senāsana-gilānappaccaya-bhesajja-parikkhāraṁ tesaṁ te kārā amhesu
31 And all our use of robes, almsfood, lodgings, and medicinal requisites, given by others for our support,
# Mahapphalā bhavissanti mahānisaṁsā'ti
32 Will reward them with great fruit and great benefit.'
# Evaṁ hi vo bhikkhave sikkhitabbaṁ
33 Bhikkhus, you should train yourselves thus:
# Att'atthaṁ vā hi bhikkhave sampassamānena
34 Considering your own good,
# Alam-eva appamādena sampādetuṁ
35 It is enough to strive for the goal without negligence;
# Par'atthaṁ vā hi bhikkhave sampassamānena
36 Bhikkhus, considering the good of others,
# Alam-eva appamādena sampādetuṁ
37 It is enough to strive for the goal without negligence;
# Ubhay'atthaṁ vā hi bhikkhave sampassamānena
38 Bhikkhus, considering the good of both,
# Alam-eva appamādena sampādetun'ti
39 It is enough to strive for the goal without negligence.
