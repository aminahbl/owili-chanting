[chant:0037 sabba-patti-dana-gatha]
# Puññass'idāni katassa
1 May whatever living beings,
# Yān'aññāni katāni me
2 Without measure, without end,
# Tesañca bhāgino hontu
3 Partake of all the merit,
# Sattānantāppamāṇakā
4 From the good deeds I have done:
# Ye piyā guṇavantā ca
5 Those loved and full of goodness,
# Mayhaṁ mātā-pitādayo
6 My mother and my father dear,
# Diṭṭhā me cāpyadiṭṭhā vā
7 Beings seen by me and those unseen,
# Aññe majjhatta-verino
8 Those neutral and averse,
# Sattā tiṭṭhanti lokasmiṁ
9 Beings established in the world,
# Te bhummā catu-yonikā
10 From the three planes and four grounds of birth,
# Pañc'eka-catu-vokārā
11 With five aggregates or one or four,
# Saṁsarantā bhavābhave
12 Wand'ring on from realm to realm,
# Ñātaṁ ye patti-dānam-me
13 Those who know my act of dedication,
# Anumodantu te sayaṁ
14 May they all rejoice in it,
# Ye c'imaṁ nappajānanti
15 And as for those yet unaware,
# Devā tesaṁ nivedayuṁ
16 May the devas let them know.
# Mayā dinnāna-puññānaṁ anumodana-hetunā
17 By rejoicing in my sharing,
# Sabbe sattā sadā hontu
18 May all beings live at ease,
# Averā sukha-jīvino
19 In freedom from hostility,
# Khemappadañca pappontu
20 May their good wishes be fulfilled,
# Tesāsā sijjhataṁ subhā
21 And may they all reach safety.
