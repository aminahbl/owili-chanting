[chant:0049 invitation-for-paritta-chanting]
# Vipatti-paṭibāhāya sabba-sampatti-siddhiyā
1 For warding off misfortune, for the arising of good fortune,
# Sabbadukkha-vināsāya
2 For the dispelling of all dukkha,
# Parittaṁ brūtha maṅgalaṁ
3 May you chant a blessing and protection.
# Vipatti-paṭibāhāya sabba-sampatti-siddhiyā
4 For warding off misfortune, for the arising of good fortune,
# Sabbabhaya-vināsāya
5 For the dispelling of all fear,
# Parittaṁ brūtha maṅgalaṁ
6 May you chant a blessing and protection.
# Vipatti-paṭibāhāya sabba-sampatti-siddhiyā
7 For warding off misfortune, for the arising of good fortune,
# Sabbaroga-vināsāya
8 For the dispelling of all sickness,
# Parittaṁ brūtha maṅgalaṁ
9 May you chant a blessing and protection.
