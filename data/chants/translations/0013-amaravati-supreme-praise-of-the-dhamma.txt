[chant:0013 supreme-praise-of-the-dhamma]
# Svākkhātat'ādiguṇa-yoga-vasena seyyo
1 It is excellent because it is 'well expounded,'
# Yo magga-pāka-pariyatti-vimokkha-bhedo
2 And it can be divided into Path and Fruit, Learning and Liberation.
# Dhammo kuloka-patanā tada-dhāri-dhārī
3 The Dhamma holds those who uphold it from falling into delusion.
# Vandām'ahaṁ tama-haraṁ vara-dhammam-etaṁ
4 I revere the excellent Teaching, that which removes darkness —
# Dhammo yo sabba-pāṇīnaṁ saraṇaṁ khemam-uttamaṁ
5 The Dhamma, which is the supreme, secure refuge of all beings —
# Dutiyānussatiṭṭhānaṁ vandāmi taṁ siren'ahaṁ
6 As the Second Object of Recollection, I venerate it with bowed head.
# Dhammassāh'asmi dāso va dhammo me sāmi-kissaro
7 I am indeed the Dhamma's servant, the Dhamma is my Lord and Guide.
# Dhammo dukkhassa ghātā ca vidhātā ca hitassa me
8 The Dhamma is sorrow's destroyer, and it bestows blessings on me.
# Dhammass'āhaṁ niyyādemi sarīrañ-jīvitañ-cidaṁ
9 To the Dhamma I dedicate this body and life,
# Vandantohaṁ carissāmi dhammass'eva sudhammataṁ
10 And in devotion I will walk this excellent way of Truth.
# Natthi me saraṇaṁ aññaṁ dhammo me saraṇaṁ varaṁ
11 For me there is no other refuge, the Dhamma is my excellent refuge.
# Etena sacca-vajjena vaḍḍheyyaṁ satthu-sāsane
12 By the utterance of this Truth, may I grow in the Master's Way.
# Dhammaṁ me vandamānena yaṁ puññaṁ pasutaṁ idha
13 By my devotion to the Dhamma, and the blessing of this practice —
# Sabbepi antarāyā me māhesuṁ tassa tejasā
14 By its power, may all obstacles be overcome.
# Kāyena vācāya va cetasā vā
16 By body, speech, or mind,
# Dhamme kukammaṁ pakataṁ mayā yaṁ
17 For whatever wrong action I have committed towards the Dhamma,
# Dhammo paṭiggaṇhātu accayantaṁ
18 May my acknowledgement of fault be accepted,
# Kālantare saṁvarituṁ va dhamme
19 That in future there may be restraint regarding the Dhamma.
