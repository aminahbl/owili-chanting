[chant:0121 yamaka]
# Ye keci kusalā dhammā, sabbe te kusalamūlā.
1 Whatever are wholesome things, are they all wholesome roots?
# Ye vā pana kusalamūlā, sabbe te dhammā kusalā.
2 Whatever are wholesome roots, are they all wholesome things?
# Ye keci kusalā dhammā, sabbe te kusalamūlena ekamūlā.
3 Whatever are wholesome things, do they all have the same roots with the wholesome root?
# Ye vā pana kusalamūlena ekamūlā, sabbe te dhammā kusalā.
4 Whatever have the same roots with the wholesome root, are they all wholesome roots?
