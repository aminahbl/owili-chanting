[chant:0003 dedication-of-offerings]
# {Yo so} bhagavā arahaṁ sammāsambuddho
1 {He who is} the Fortunate One, the Worthy One, the Perfect Sambuddha,
# Svākkhāto yena bhagavatā dhammo
2 the Dhamma which has been well-proclaimed by the Fortunate One,
# Supaṭipanno yassa bhagavato sāvakasaṅgho
3 the Fortunate One’s Saṅgha of disciples who are good in their practice:
# Tam-mayaṁ bhagavantaṁ sadhammaṁ sasaṅghaṁ
4 the Fortunate One, together with the Dhamma and the Saṅgha,
# Imehi sakkārehi yathārahaṁ āropitehi abhipūjayāma
5 we worship, as is worthy, together with the respects that are prescribed.
# Sādhu no bhante bhagavā sucira-parinibbutopi
6 Although our venerable Fortunate One has attained Nibbāna a very long time ago,
# Pacchimā-janatānukampa-mānasā
7 out of a compassionate mind for later generations,
# Ime sakkāre duggata-paṇṇākāra-bhūte paṭiggaṇhātu
8 please accept these poor offerings, given with respect,
# Amhākaṁ dīgharattaṁ hitāya sukhāya
9 for our benefit and happiness for a long time.
# Arahaṁ sammāsambuddho bhagavā
10 The Fortunate One is Worthy, the Perfect Sambuddha:
# Buddhaṁ bhagavantaṁ abhivādemi
11 I worship the Buddha, the Fortunate One.
# {Svākkhāto} bhagavatā dhammo
12 The Dhamma has been well-proclaimed by the Fortunate One:
# Dhammaṁ namassāmi
13 I honour the Dhamma.
# {Supaṭipanno} bhagavato sāvakasaṅgho
14 The Fortunate One’s Saṅgha of disciples are good in their practice:
# Saṅghaṁ namāmi
15 I revere the Saṅgha.
