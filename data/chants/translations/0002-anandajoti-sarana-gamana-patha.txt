[chant:0002 sarana-gamana-patha]
# Buddhaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi
1 I go to the Buddha for refuge
# Dhammaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi
2 I go to the Dhamma for refuge
# Saṅghaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi
3 I go to the Saṅgha for refuge
# Dutiyam pi buddhaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi
4 For a second time I go to the Buddha for refuge
# Dutiyam pi dhammaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi
5 For a second time I go to the Dhamma for refuge
# Dutiyam pi saṅghaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi
6 For a second time I go to the Saṅgha for refuge
# Tatiyam pi buddhaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi
7 For a third time I go to the Buddha for refuge
# Tatiyam pi dhammaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi
8 For a third time I go to the Dhamma for refuge
# Tatiyam pi saṅghaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi
9 For a third time I go to the Saṅgha for refuge
