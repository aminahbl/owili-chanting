[chant:0011 supreme-praise-of-the-buddha]
# Buddh'vārahanta-varatādiguṇābhiyutto
1 The Buddha has noble virtues, such as being Worthy and so forth,
# Suddhābhiñāṇa-karuṇāhi samāgatatto
2 together with pure higher knowledges and compassion,
# Bodhesi yo sujanataṁ kamalaṁ va sūro
3 the one who awakens good people like the sun (awakens) the lotus,
# Vandām'ahaṁ tam-araṇaṁ sirasā jinendaṁ
4 I worship that passionless, Victorious Lord with my head.
# Buddho yo sabba-pāṇīnaṁ saraṇaṁ khemam-uttamaṁ
5 The Buddha, who is the supreme refuge for all living beings,
# Paṭhamānussatiṭṭhānaṁ vandāmi taṁ siren'ahaṁ
6 I recall first in this place, I worship him with my head,
# Buddhassāh'asmi dāso va buddho me sāmi-kissaro
7 I am the servant of the Buddha, the Buddha is my lord and master.
# Buddho dukkhassa ghātā ca vidhātā ca hitassa me
8 The Buddha is a destroyer of suffering, a provider (of solace) and he benefits me.
# Buddhass'āhaṁ niyyādemi sarīrañ-jīvitañ-cidaṁ
9 I dedicate this body and this life to the Buddha.
# Vandanto'haṁ carissāmi buddhass'eva subodhitaṁ
10 I will live worshipping the Buddha’s True Awakening.
# Natthi me saraṇaṁ aññaṁ buddho me saraṇaṁ varaṁ
11 For me there is no other refuge, for me the Buddha is the best refuge!
# Etena sacca-vajjena vaḍḍheyyaṁ satthu-sāsane
12 By this declaration of the truth may the Teacher’s dispensation prosper.
# Buddhaṁ me vandamānena yaṁ puññaṁ pasutaṁ idha
13 Through my worship of the Buddha and whatever merit is found here,
# Sabbepi antarāyā me māhesuṁ tassa tejasā
14 through the power of that, may there be no dangers for me.
# Kāyena vācāya va cetasā vā
16 By body, by speech or by mind,
# Buddhe kukammaṁ pakataṁ mayā yaṁ
17 whatever bad deeds were done by me regarding the Buddha,
# Buddho paṭiggaṇhātu accayantaṁ
18 may the Buddha accept my confession,
# Kālantare saṁvarituṁ va buddhe
19 for my restraint in the future regarding the Buddha.
