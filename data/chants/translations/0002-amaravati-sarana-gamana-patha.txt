[chant:0002 sarana-gamana-patha]
# Buddhaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi
1 To the Buddha I go for refuge.
# Dhammaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi
2 To the Dhamma I go for refuge.
# Saṅghaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi
3 To the Saṅgha I go for refuge.
# Dutiyam pi buddhaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi
4 For the second time, to the Buddha I go for refuge.
# Dutiyam pi dhammaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi
5 For the second time, to the Dhamma I go for refuge.
# Dutiyam pi saṅghaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi
6 For the second time, to the Saṅgha I go for refuge.
# Tatiyam pi buddhaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi
7 For the third time, to the Buddha I go for refuge.
# Tatiyam pi dhammaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi
8 For the third time, to the Dhamma I go for refuge.
# Tatiyam pi saṅghaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi
9 For the third time, to the Saṅgha I go for refuge.
