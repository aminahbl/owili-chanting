[chant:0089 anapanassati-sutta]
# Ānāpānassati bhikkhave bhāvitā bahulīkatā
1 Bhikkhus, when mindfulness of breathing is developed and cultivated
# Mahapphalā hoti mahānisaṁsā
2 It is of great fruit and great benefit;
# Ānāpānassati bhikkhave bhāvitā bahulīkatā
3 When mindfulness of breathing is developed and cultivated
# Cattāro satipaṭṭhāne paripūreti
4 It fulfills the Four Foundations of Mindfulness;
# Cattāro satipaṭṭhānā bhāvitā bahulīkatā
5 When the Four Foundations of Mindfulness are developed and cultivated
# Satta-bojjhaṅge paripūrenti
6 They fulfill the Seven Factors of Awakening;
# Satta-bojjhaṅgā bhāvitā bahulīkatā
7 When the Seven Factors of Awakening are developed and cultivated
# Vijjā-vimuttiṁ paripūrenti
8 They fulfill true knowledge and deliverance.
# Kathaṁ bhāvitā ca bhikkhave ānāpānassati kathaṁ bahulīkatā
9 And how, bhikkhus, is mindfulness of breathing developed and cultivated
# Mahapphalā hoti mahānisaṁsā
10 So that it is of great fruit and great benefit?
# Idha bhikkhave bhikkhu
11 Here, bhikkhus, a bhikkhu,
# Arañña-gato vā
12 Gone to the forest,
# Rukkha-mūla-gato vā
13 To the foot of a tree
# Suññāgāra-gato vā
14 Or to an empty hut.
# Nisīdati pallaṅkaṁ ābhujitvā
15 Sits down having crossed his legs,
# Ujuṁ kāyaṁ paṇidhāya parimukhaṁ satiṁ upaṭṭhapetvā
16 Sets his body erect, having established mindfulness in front of him.
# So sato'va assasati sato'va passasati
17 Ever mindful he breathes in; mindful he breathes out.
# Dīghaṁ vā assasanto dīghaṁ assasāmī'ti pajānāti
18 Breathing in long, he knows 'I breathe in long';
# Dīghaṁ vā passasanto dīghaṁ passasāmī'ti pajānāti
19 Breathing out long, he knows 'I breathe out long';
# Rassaṁ vā assasanto rassaṁ assasāmī'ti pajānāti
20 Breathing in short, he knows 'I breathe in short';
# Rassaṁ vā passasanto rassaṁ passasāmī'ti pajānāti
21 Breathing out short, he knows 'I breathe out short'.
# Sabba-kāya-paṭisaṁvedī assasissāmī'ti sikkhati
22 He trains thus: 'I shall breathe in experiencing the whole body'.
# Sabba-kāya-paṭisaṁvedī passasissāmī'ti sikkhati
23 He trains thus: 'I shall breathe out experiencing the whole body'.
# Passambhayaṁ kāya-saṅkhāraṁ assasissāmī'ti sikkhati
24 He trains thus: 'I shall breathe in tranquillizing the bodily formations'.
# Passambhayaṁ kāya-saṅkhāraṁ passasissāmī'ti sikkhati
25 He trains thus: 'I shall breathe out tranquillizing the bodily formations'.
# Pīti-paṭisaṁvedī assasissāmī'ti sikkhati
26 He trains thus: 'I shall breathe in experiencing rapture'.
# Pīti-paṭisaṁvedī passasissāmī'ti sikkhati
27 He trains thus: 'I shall breathe out experiencing rapture'.
# Sukha-paṭisaṁvedī assasissāmī'ti sikkhati
28 He trains thus: 'I shall breathe in experiencing pleasure'
# Sukha-paṭisaṁvedī passasissāmī'ti sikkhati
29 He trains thus: 'I shall breathe out experiencing pleasure'.
# Citta-saṅkhāra-paṭisaṁvedī assasissāmī'ti sikkhati
30 He trains thus: 'I shall breathe in experiencing the mental formations'.
# Citta-saṅkhāra-paṭisaṁvedī passasissāmī'ti sikkhati
31 He trains thus: 'I shall breathe out experiencing the mental formations'.
# Passambhayaṁ citta-saṅkhāraṁ assasissāmī'ti sikkhati
32 He trains thus: 'I shall breathe in tranquillizing the mental formations'.
# Passambhayaṁ citta-saṅkhāraṁ passasissāmī'ti sikkhati
33 He trains thus: 'I shall breathe out tranquillizing the mental formations'.
# Citta-paṭisaṁvedī assasissāmī'ti sikkhati
34 He trains thus: 'I shall breathe in experiencing the mind'.
# Citta-paṭisaṁvedī passasissāmī'ti sikkhati
35 He trains thus: 'I shall breathe out experiencing the mind'.
# Abhippamodayaṁ cittaṁ assasissāmī'ti sikkhati
36 He trains thus: 'I shall breathe in gladdening the mind'.
# Abhippamodayaṁ cittaṁ passasissāmī'ti sikkhati
37 He trains thus: 'I shall breathe out gladdening the mind'.
# Samādahaṁ cittaṁ assasissāmī'ti sikkhati
38 He trains thus: 'I shall breathe in concentrating the mind'
# Samādahaṁ cittaṁ passasissāmī'ti sikkhati
39 He trains thus: 'I shall breathe out concentrating the mind'.
# Vimocayaṁ cittaṁ assasissāmī'ti sikkhati
40 He trains thus: 'I shall breathe in liberating the mind'.
# Vimocayaṁ cittaṁ passasissāmī'ti sikkhati
41 He trains thus: 'I shall breathe out liberating the mind'.
# Aniccānupassī assasissāmī'ti sikkhati
42 He trains thus: 'I shall breathe in contemplating impermanence'.
# Aniccānupassī passasissāmī'ti sikkhati
43 He trains thus: 'I shall breathe out contemplating impermanence'.
# Virāgānupassī assasissāmī'ti sikkhati
44 He trains thus: 'I shall breathe in contemplating the fading away of passions'.
# Virāgānupassī passasissāmī'ti sikkhati
45 He trains thus: 'I shall breathe out contemplating the fading away of passions'.
# Nirodhānupassī assasissāmī'ti sikkhati
46 He trains thus: 'I shall breathe in contemplating cessation'.
# Nirodhānupassī passasissāmī'ti sikkhati
47 He trains thus: 'I shall breathe out contemplating cessation'.
# Paṭinissaggānupassī assasissāmī'ti sikkhati
48 He trains thus: 'I shall breathe in contemplating relinquishment'.
# Paṭinissaggānupassī passasissāmī'ti sikkhati
49 He trains thus: 'I shall breathe out contemplating relinquishment'.
# Evaṁ bhāvitā kho bhikkhave ānāpānassati evaṁ bahulīkatā
50 Bhikkhus, that is how mindfulness of breathing is developed and cultivated
# Mahapphalā hoti mahānisaṁsā'ti
51 So that it is of great fruit and great benefit.
