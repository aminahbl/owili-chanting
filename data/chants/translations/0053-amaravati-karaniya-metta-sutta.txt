[chant:0053 karaniya-metta-sutta]
# Karaṇīyam-attha-kusalena
1 {This is what should be done} By one who is skilled in goodness
# Yan-taṁ santaṁ padaṁ abhisamecca
2 And who knows the path of peace:
# Sakko ujū ca suhujū ca
3 Let them be able and upright,
# Suvaco c'assa mudu anatimānī
4 Straightforward and gentle in speech, Humble and not conceited,
# Santussako ca subharo ca
5 Contented and easily satisfied,
# Appakicco ca sallahuka-vutti
6 Unburdened with duties and frugal in their ways.
# Sant'indriyo ca nipako ca
7 Peaceful and calm, and wise and skilful,
# Appagabbho kulesu ananugiddho
8 Not proud and demanding in nature.
# Na ca khuddaṁ samācare kiñci
9 Let them not do the slightest thing
# Yena viññū pare upavadeyyuṁ
10 That the wise would later reprove,
# Sukhino vā khemino hontu
11 Wishing: In gladness and in safety,
# Sabbe sattā bhavantu sukhit'attā
12 May all beings be at ease.
# Ye keci pāṇa-bhūt'atthi
13 Whatever living beings there may be,
# Tasā vā thāvarā vā anavasesā
14 Whether they are weak or strong, omitting none,
# Dīghā vā ye mahantā vā
15 The great or the mighty,
# Majjhimā rassakā aṇuka-thūlā
16 medium, short, or small,
# Diṭṭhā vā ye ca adiṭṭhā
17 The seen and the unseen,
# Ye ca dūre vasanti avidūre
18 Those living near and far away,
# Bhūtā vā sambhavesī vā
19 Those born and to be born,
# Sabbe sattā bhavantu sukhit'attā
20 May all beings be at ease.
# Na paro paraṁ nikubbetha
21 Let none deceive another
# Nātimaññetha katthaci naṁ kiñci
22 Or despise any being in any state.
# Byārosanā paṭighasaññā
23 Let none through anger or ill-will
# Nāññam-aññassa dukkham-iccheyya
24 Wish harm upon another.
# Mātā yathā niyaṁ puttaṁ
25 Even as a mother protects with her life
# Āyusā eka-puttam-anurakkhe
26 Her child, her only child,
# Evam'pi sabba-bhūtesu
27 So with a boundless heart
# Mānasam-bhāvaye aparimāṇaṁ
28 Should one cherish all living beings,
# Mettañ-ca sabba-lokasmiṁ
30 Radiating kindness
# Mānasam-bhāvaye aparimāṇaṁ
31 over the entire world:
# Uddhaṁ adho ca tiriyañ-ca
32 Spreading upwards to the skies And downwards to the depths, Outwards
# Asambādhaṁ averaṁ asapattaṁ
33 and unbounded, Freed from hatred and ill-will.
# Tiṭṭhañ-caraṁ nisinno vā
34 Whether standing or walking, seated,
# Sayāno vā yāvat'assa vigata-middho
35 Or lying down — free from drowsiness —
# Etaṁ satiṁ adhiṭṭheyya
36 One should sustain this recollection.
# Brahmam-etaṁ vihāraṁ idham-āhu
37 This is said to be the sublime abiding.
# Diṭṭhiñca anupagamma
38 By not holding to fixed views,
# Sīlavā dassanena sampanno
39 The pure-hearted one, having clarity of vision,
# Kāmesu vineyya gedhaṁ
40 Being freed from all sense-desires,
# Na hi jātu gabbha-seyyaṁ punaretī'ti
41 Is not born again into this world.
