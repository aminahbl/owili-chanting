[chant:0019 buddha-dhamma-sangha-guna]
# Iti pi so bhagavā arahaṁ sammā-sambuddho
1 Such is he, the Fortunate One, the Worthy One, the Perfect Sambuddha,
# Vijjā-caraṇa-sampanno sugato loka-vidū
2 the one endowed with understanding and good conduct, the Happy One, the one who understands the worlds,
# Anuttaro purisa-damma-sārathi
3 the unsurpassed guide for those people who need taming,
# Satthā devamanussānaṁ buddho bhagavā'ti
4 the Teacher of gods and men, the Buddha, the Fortunate One.
# Svākkhāto bhagavatā dhammo sandiṭṭhiko
5 The Dhamma has been well-proclaimed by the Fortunate One, it is visible,
# akāliko ehi-passiko opanayiko
6 not subject to time, inviting inspection, onward leading,
# paccattaṁ veditabbo viññūhī'ti
7 and can be understood by the wise for themselves.
# Supaṭipanno bhagavato sāvaka-saṅgho
8 The Fortunate One’s Saṅgha of disciples are good in their practice,
# Uju-paṭipanno bhagavato sāvaka-saṅgho
9 the Fortunate One’s Saṅgha of disciples are upright in their practice,
# Ñāya-paṭipanno bhagavato sāvaka-saṅgho
10 the Fortunate One’s Saṅgha of disciples are systematic in their practice,
# Sāmīci-paṭipanno bhagavato sāvaka-saṅgho
11 the Fortunate One’s Saṅgha of disciples are correct in their practice,
# Yad-idaṁ cattāri purisa-yugāni aṭṭha purisa-puggalā
12 that is to say, the four pairs of persons, the eight individual persons,
# Esa bhagavato sāvaka-saṅgho
13 this is the Fortunate One’s Saṅgha of disciples,
# Āhuneyyo pāhuneyyo dakkhiṇeyyo añjali-karaṇīyo
14 they are worthy of offerings, of hospitality, of gifts, and of reverential salutation,
# Anuttaraṁ puññakkhettaṁ lokassā'ti
15 they are an unsurpassed field of merit for the world.
