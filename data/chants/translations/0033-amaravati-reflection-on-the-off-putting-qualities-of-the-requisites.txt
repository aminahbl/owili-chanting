[chant:0033 reflection-on-the-off-putting-qualities-of-the-requisites]
# {Yathā paccayaṁ} pavattamānaṁ dhātu-mattam-ev'etaṁ
1 Composed of only elements according to causes and conditions
# Yad idaṁ cīvaraṁ tad upabhuñjako ca puggalo
2 Are these robes and so is the person wearing them;
# Dhātu-mattako,
3 Merely elements,
# Nissatto,
4 Not a being,
# Nijjīvo,
5 Without a soul
# Suñño
6 And empty of self.
# Sabbāni pana imāni cīvarāni ajigucchanīyāni
7 None of these robes are innately repulsive
# Imaṁ pūti-kāyaṁ patvā,
8 But touching this unclean body
# Ativiya jigucchanīyāni jāyanti
9 They become disgusting indeed.
# Yathā paccayaṁ pavattamānaṁ dhātu-mattam-ev'etaṁ
10 Composed of only elements according to causes and conditions
# Yad idaṁ piṇḍapāto tad upabhuñjako ca puggalo
11 Is this almsfood and so is the person eating it;
# Dhātu-mattako,
12 Merely elements,
# Nissatto,
13 Not a being,
# Nijjīvo,
14 Without a soul
# Suñño
15 And empty of self.
# Sabbo panāyaṁ piṇḍapāto ajigucchanīyo
16 None of this almsfood is innately repulsive
# Imaṁ pūti-kāyaṁ patvā,
17 But touching this unclean body
# Ativiya jigucchanīyo jāyati
18 It becomes disgusting indeed.
# Yathā paccayaṁ pavattamānaṁ dhātu-mattam-ev'etaṁ
19 Composed of only elements according to causes and conditions
# Yad idaṁ senāsanaṁ tad upabhuñjako ca puggalo
20 Is this dwelling and so is the person using it;
# Dhātu-mattako,
21 Merely elements,
# Nissatto,
22 Not a being,
# Nijjīvo,
23 Without a soul
# Suñño
24 And empty of self.
# Sabbāni pana imāni senāsanāni ajigucchanīyāni
25 None of these dwellings are innately repulsive
# Imaṁ pūti-kāyaṁ patvā,
26 But touching this unclean body
# Ativiya jigucchanīyāni jāyanti
27 They become disgusting indeed.
# Yathā paccayaṁ pavattamānaṁ dhātu-mattam-ev'etaṁ
28 Composed of only elements according to causes and conditions
# Yad idaṁ gilāna-paccaya-bhesajja-parikkhāro tad upabhuñjako ca puggalo
29 Is this medicinal requisite and so is the person that takes it;
# Dhātu-mattako,
30 Merely elements,
# Nissatto,
31 Not a being,
# Nijjīvo,
32 Without a soul
# Suñño
33 And empty of self.
# Sabbo panāyaṁ gilāna-paccaya-bhesajja-parikkhāro ajigucchanīyo
34 None of this medicinal requisite is innately repulsive
# Imaṁ pūti-kāyaṁ patvā,
35 But touching this unclean body
# Ativiya jigucchanīyo jāyati
36 It becomes disgusting indeed.
