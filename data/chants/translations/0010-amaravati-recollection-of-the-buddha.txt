[chant:0010 recollection-of-the-buddha]
# Taṁ kho pana bhagavantaṁ evaṁ kalyāṇo kittisaddo abbhuggato
1 A good word of the Blessed One's reputation has spread as follows:
# Itipi so bhagavā arahaṁ sammāsambuddho
2 He, the Blessed One, is indeed the Pure One, the Perfectly Enlightened One;
# Vijjācaraṇa-sampanno sugato lokavidū
3 He is impeccable in conduct and understanding, the Accomplished One, the Knower of the Worlds;
# Anuttaro purisadamma-sārathi satthā deva-manussānaṁ buddho bhagavā'ti
4 He trains perfectly those who wish to be trained; he is Teacher of gods and humans; he is Awake and Holy.
