[chant:0113 ariya-dhana-gatha]
# Yassa saddhā tathāgate
1 One whose faith in the Tathāgata
# acalā supatiṭṭhitā
2 Is unshaken and established well,
# Sīlañ-ca yassa kalyāṇaṁ
3 Whose virtue is beautiful,
# ariya-kantaṁ pasaṁsitaṁ
4 The Noble Ones enjoy and praise;
# Saṅghe pasādo yass'atthi
5 Whose trust is in the Saṅgha,
# uju-bhūtañ-ca dassanaṁ
6 Who sees things rightly as they are,
# Adaliddo-ti taṁ āhu
7 It is said that not in vain
# amoghaṁ tassa jīvitaṁ
8 And undeluded is their life.
# Tasmā saddhañ-ca sīlañ-ca
9 To virtue and to faith,
# pasādaṁ dhamma-dassanaṁ
10 To trust to seeing truth,
# Anuyuñjetha medhāvī
11 To these the wise devote themselves,
# saraṁ buddhāna sāsanan'ti
12 The Buddha's teaching in their mind.
