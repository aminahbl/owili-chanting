[chant:0038 uddissanadhitthana-gatha]
# {Iminā puññakammena} upajjhāyā guṇuttarā
1 Through the goodness that arises from my practice, May my spiritual teachers and guides of great virtue,
# Ācariyūpakārā ca mātāpitā ca ñātakā
2 My mother, my father, and my relatives,
# Suriyo candimā rājā guṇavantā narāpi ca
3 The Sun and the Moon, and all virtuous leaders of the world,
# Brahma-mārā ca indā ca lokapālā ca devatā
4 May the highest gods and evil forces, Celestial beings, guardian spirits of the Earth,
# Yamo mittā manussā ca majjhattā verikāpi ca
5 and the Lord of Death, May those who are friendly, indifferent, or hostile,
# Sabbe sattā sukhī hontu puññāni pakatāni me
6 May all beings receive the blessings of my life,
# Sukhañca tividhaṁ dentu khippaṁ pāpetha vomataṁ
7 May they soon attain the threefold bliss and realize the Deathless.
# Iminā puññakammena iminā uddissena ca
8 Through the goodness that arises from my practice, And through this act of sharing,
# Khipp'āhaṁ sulabhe ceva taṇhūpādāna-chedanaṁ
9 May all cravings and attachments quickly cease
# Ye santāne hīnā dhammā yāva nibbānato mamaṁ
10 And all harmful states of mind. Until I realize Nibbāna,
# Nassantu sabbadā yeva yattha jāto bhave bhave
11 In every kind of birth,
# Ujucittaṁ satipaññā sallekho viriyamhinā
12 may I have an upright mind, With mindfulness and wisdom, austerity and vigour.
# Mārā labhantu nokāsaṁ kātuñca viriyesu me
13 May the forces of delusion not take hold nor weaken my resolve.
# Buddhādhipavaro nātho dhammo nātho varuttamo
14 The Buddha is my excellent refuge, Unsurpassed is the protection of the Dhamma,
# Nātho paccekabuddho ca saṅgho nāthottaro mamaṁ
15 The Solitary Buddha is my noble guide, The Saṅgha is my supreme support.
# Tesottamānubhāvena mārokāsaṁ labhantu mā
16 Through the supreme power of all these, May darkness and delusion be dispelled.
