[chant:0008 salutation-to-the-triple-gem]
# Buddho susuddho karuṇā-mahaṇṇavo
1 The Buddha, absolutely pure, with ocean-like compassion,
# Yo'ccanta-suddhabbara-ñāṇa-locano
2 Possessing the clear sight of wisdom,
# Lokassa pāpūpakilesa-ghātako
3 Destroyer of worldly self-corruption —
# Vandāmi buddhaṁ aham-ādarena taṁ
4 Devotedly indeed, that Buddha I revere.
# Dhammo padīpo viya tassa satthuno
5 The Teaching of the Lord, like a lamp,
# Yo magga-pākāmata-bheda-bhinnako
6 Illuminating the Path and its Fruit: the Deathless,
# Lokuttaro yo ca tad-attha-dīpano
7 That which is beyond the conditioned world —
# Vandāmi dhammaṁ aham-ādarena taṁ
8 Devotedly indeed, that Dhamma I revere.
# Saṅgho sukhettābhyati-khetta-saññito
9 The Saṅgha, the most fertile ground for cultivation,
# Yo diṭṭha-santo sugatānubodhako
10 Those who have realized peace, awakened after the Accomplished One,
# Lolappahīno ariyo sumedhaso
11 Noble and wise, all longing abandoned —
# Vandāmi saṅghaṁ aham-ādarena taṁ
12 Devotedly indeed, that Saṅgha I revere.
# Iccevam-ekantabhipūja-neyyakaṁ vatthuttayaṁ vandayatābhisaṅkhataṁ
13 This salutation should be made to that which is worthy.
# Puññaṁ mayā yaṁ mama sabbupaddavā mā hontu ve tassa pabhāva-siddhiyā
14 Through the power of such good action, may all obstacles disappear.
# Idha tathāgato loke uppanno arahaṁ sammāsambuddho
15 One who knows things as they are has come into this world; and he is an Arahant, a perfectly Awakened being,
# Dhammo ca desito niyyāniko upasamiko parinibbāniko sambodhagāmī sugatappavedito
16 Purifying the way leading out of delusion, calming and directing to perfect peace, and leading to enlightenment — this Way he has made known.
# Mayan-taṁ dhammaṁ sutvā evaṁ jānāma
17 Having heard the Teaching, we know this:
# Jātipi dukkhā
18 Birth is dukkha,
# Jarāpi dukkhā
19 Ageing is dukkha,
# Maraṇampi dukkhaṁ
20 And death is dukkha;
# Soka-parideva-dukkha-domanass'upāyāsāpi dukkhā
21 Sorrow, lamentation, pain, grief, and despair are dukkha;
# Appiyehi sampayogo dukkho
22 Association with the disliked is dukkha;
# Piyehi vippayogo dukkho
23 Separation from the liked is dukkha;
# Yamp'icchaṁ na labhati tampi dukkhaṁ
24 Not attaining one's wishes is dukkha.
# Saṅkhittena pañcupādānakkhandhā dukkhā
25 In brief, the five focuses of identity are dukkha.
# Seyyathīdaṁ
26 These are as follows:
# Rūpūpādānakkhandho
27 Attachment to form,
# Vedanūpādānakkhandho
28 Attachment to feeling,
# Saññūpādānakkhandho
29 Attachment to perception,
# Saṅkhārūpādānakkhandho
30 Attachment to mental formations,
# Viññāṇūpādānakkhandho
31 Attachment to sense-consciousness.
# Yesaṁ pariññāya
32 For the complete understanding of this,
# Dharamāno so bhagavā evaṁ bahulaṁ sāvake vineti
33 The Blessed One in his lifetime frequently instructed his disciples in just this way.
# Evaṁ bhāgā ca panassa bhagavato sāvakesu anusāsanī bahulā pavattati
34 In addition, he further instructed:
# Rūpaṁ aniccaṁ
35 Form is impermanent,
# Vedanā aniccā
36 Feeling is impermanent,
# Saññā aniccā
37 Perception is impermanent,
# Saṅkhārā aniccā
38 Mental formations are impermanent,
# Viññāṇaṁ aniccaṁ
39 Sense-consciousness is impermanent;
# Rūpaṁ anattā
40 Form is not-self,
# Vedanā anattā
41 Feeling is not-self,
# Saññā anattā
42 Perception is not-self,
# Saṅkhārā anattā
43 Mental formations are not-self,
# Viññāṇaṁ anattā
44 Sense-consciousness is not-self;
# Sabbe saṅkhārā aniccā
45 All conditions are transient,
# Sabbe dhammā anattā'ti
46 There is no self in the created or the uncreated.
# Te mayaṁ otiṇṇāmha jātiyā jarā-maraṇena
47 All of us are bound by birth, ageing, and death,
# Sokehi paridevehi dukkhehi domanassehi upāyāsehi
48 By sorrow, lamentation, pain, grief, and despair,
# Dukkhotiṇṇā dukkha-paretā
49 Bound by dukkha and obstructed by dukkha.
# Appeva nāmimassa kevalassa dukkha-kkhandhassa antakiriyā paññāyethā'ti
50 Let us all aspire to complete freedom from suffering.
# Cira-parinibbutampi taṁ bhagavantaṁ uddissa arahantaṁ sammāsambuddhaṁ
52 Remembering the Blessed One, the Noble Lord, and Perfectly Enlightened One, who long ago attained Parinibbāna,
# Saddhā agārasmā anagāriyaṁ pabbajitā
53 We have gone forth with faith from home to homelessness,
# Tasmiṁ bhagavati brahma-cariyaṁ carāma
54 And like the Blessed One, we practise the Holy Life,
# Bhikkhūnaṁ sikkhāsājīva-samāpannā
55 Being fully equipped with the bhikkhus'/nuns' system of training.
# Taṁ no brahma-cariyaṁ imassa kevalassa dukkha-kkhandhassa antakiriyāya saṁvattatu
56 May this Holy Life lead us to the end of this whole mass of suffering.
